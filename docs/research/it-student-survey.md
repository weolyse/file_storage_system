# IT student file-workflow survey

Created and published: 23 October 2026. Language: English. Estimated completion time: 5–7 minutes.

[Respondent form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd7ZpdndCcaqC3zAMaIH9kQs-vLVP6lGYi9pxuwHBLJSFwC0w/viewform?usp=header) · [Edit form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/19KODYuvjlcS5RRJbwLa1mvipRUSrIaTXwUss3bCBuME/edit)

Purpose: collect self-reported user evidence for section 2 of the diploma analysis. The form is an instrument, not research findings. Zero submitted responses were shown at creation; the browser path was checked without submitting a test response.

## Participation and settings

Participation is voluntary. Q1 consent and Q2 IT-student eligibility are required. “I do not agree” or “No” routes to the end; exclude these records from the substantive sample. All substantive questions are optional. Email collection, sign-in-based one-response restriction and public response summaries are off. Respondents are asked not to enter personal details or private file contents. No file upload is requested. Q14 permits at most three selections. Only the respondent link should be distributed; the edit link remains subject to account permissions.

## Analysis plan

- Recruit a convenience sample of current IT students across years, rather than only team members. A practical initial target is 30 usable responses; this is a recruitment target, not a representative-sample guarantee. Record recruitment channel, collection dates and actual sample size. The survey has not been distributed by the agent.
- Include only consenting IT students. Report denominators per question because substantive items are optional. Distinguish skipped answers, explicit uncertainty, no uploads and no issues. Do not impute missing answers.
- Report frequency distributions for Q4/Q8/Q11/Q12/Q15/Q16/Q18. Report counts and percentages for checkbox items; multi-select percentages may exceed 100%. Flag contradictory exclusive options (e.g. “None” plus a tool), preserve raw answers and document any exclusion at item level.
- Use Q4–Q8 to describe actual workflows and review file-type/size assumptions (FR-03, NFR-03). Sizes are approximate self-reports, not byte-accurate measurements.
- Use Q9–Q12 to assess whether practical upload/processing problems occurred and their consequences. Q13 is a hypothetical preference; it is not measured behaviour or evidence of distributed-system performance.
- Use Q14–Q16 to review feature priorities, ownership/privacy (FR-02, NFR-01) and satisfaction with current alternatives. Q18 identifies respondents with integration experience.
- Code Q10/Q17 into themes with anonymized excerpts. Retain positive feedback, “nothing to change” and disagreement. A second team member should review the coding.
- Add findings to analysis section 2 only after collecting actual data. Use a finding → question/count → limitation → requirement/decision table. Convenience sampling and recall bias limit generalization; do not claim causality or validate performance from opinions.
- Supplement the survey with the 3–5 actual discussions or substantive client agreement required by the assessment. The questionnaire alone does not establish that these discussions occurred.
